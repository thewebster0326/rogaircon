Aircon servicing & regas

How we approach it

In a humid city, aircons pull a lot of water out of the air. That water feeds mould on dirty coils and blocks drains, which is where most leaks and smells come from. A service clears it before it becomes a repair.

Units that run all day in offices, shops and restaurants should be serviced every six months; home units usually once a year.

Signs you need us

  • Musty smell when the aircon starts
  • Weaker airflow than it used to have
  • Takes much longer to cool the room
  • Higher electricity bills in summer
  • Water starting to drip from the indoor unit
  • No service in the last year

What we repair

  • Filters cleaned or replaced
  • Indoor coil and blower wheel cleaned
  • Outdoor condenser coil cleaned
  • Drain line and tray cleared and flushed
  • Gas pressures and temperatures checked
  • Electrical connections and operation tested

Questions

Aircon servicing: common questions

How often should an aircon be serviced in Durban?

Every six months for units that run all day in offices, shops and restaurants. Once a year for most homes. Coastal units benefit from more frequent outdoor coil cleaning.

Does servicing really lower power bills?

Yes. Dirty filters and coils make the unit run longer to reach the same temperature. A clean unit cools faster and cycles off sooner.

Can you service several units at once?

Yes. For offices and shops with several units we'll quote for servicing them all in one visit.
