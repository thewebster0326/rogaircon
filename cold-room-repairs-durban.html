Cold room & freezer room repairs

How we approach it

A cold room is a system: an insulated box, an evaporator inside, a condensing unit outside, and a controller tying them together. When the temperature creeps up, the cause can be in any of those parts, so we test the whole system rather than topping up gas and hoping.

We work on walk-in chillers and freezer rooms of all makes, from a small back-of-shop chiller to larger rooms in food production and storage.

Signs you need us

  • Room temperature creeping up, or never reaching set point
  • Thick ice on the evaporator coil inside the room
  • Water pooling on the floor or dripping from the evaporator
  • Condensing unit running non-stop, short-cycling or tripping
  • Door that won't seal, or frost forming around the frame
  • Controller showing alarms or high-temperature warnings

What we repair

  • Gas leak detection, repair and regas
  • Evaporator and condenser fan motors
  • Defrost heaters, timers and drain-line heaters
  • Thermostats, controllers and temperature probes
  • Door seals, strip curtains and door heaters
  • Compressor and contactor faults on the condensing unit

Questions

Cold room repairs: common questions

Why is there so much ice on my cold room evaporator?

Usually the defrost isn't working: a failed heater, timer or controller setting. A door left open or a torn door seal adds moisture and makes it worse. We check the defrost cycle and the seals together.

Can you come out after hours if my freezer room fails?

Call us as soon as you notice the temperature climbing. Keep the door shut and we'll tell you how quickly we can get to you.

Do you install new cold rooms as well?

Yes. Tell us the size of room and what it will store, and we'll quote for the refrigeration side of the installation.
