Our services

Find the service you need

Cold room & freezer room repairs

A walk-in cold room or freezer room that won't hold temperature can cost a butchery, restaurant or supermarket a full room of stock overnight. We find the fault and get the room cold again.

Cold room repairs

Bottle cooler repairs

Customers walk away from warm drinks. We repair single, double and triple-door glass bottle coolers in shops, spaza shops, bars and garage forecourts across Durban.

Bottle cooler repairs

Display fridge & deli counter repairs

Deli counters, meat displays, open multidecks and upright glass display fridges, repaired on site so your stock stays at a safe temperature and on show.

Display fridge repairs

Underbar & back-bar fridge repairs

When the underbar fridge goes warm on a Friday, the bar slows down. We repair under-counter and back-bar fridges in bars, pubs, restaurants and coffee shops.

Underbar fridge repairs

Compressor & condensing unit repairs

The machinery behind the cold. We diagnose and repair compressors, condensing units, evaporators and controls on commercial refrigeration systems.

Refrigeration unit repairs

Aircon installation

New split-unit air-conditioning for offices, shops, restaurants and homes, sized for the room and installed neatly, with the system vacuumed and tested before we hand over.

Aircon installation

Aircon repairs

Blowing warm air, leaking water, showing an error code or tripping the power? We repair split units and commercial air-conditioners of all makes across Durban.

Aircon repairs

Aircon servicing & regas

Durban's humidity and summer heat make regular servicing the difference between an aircon that cools well for years and one that drips, smells and fails in December.

Aircon servicing
