Who we are

Cool comfort. Fresh solutions.

We’re a Durban air-conditioning and refrigeration business working out of Kharwastan, Chatsworth. Our focus is commercial work: the cold rooms, coolers, display fridges and air-conditioners that keep shops, restaurants, supermarkets and offices running.

When a fridge full of stock stops cooling, or an office aircon packs up in the middle of a Durban summer, you need someone who answers the phone, turns up and fixes it properly. That’s the service we’ve built Rog around.

We also look after homes, so the same technicians who keep a supermarket’s cold room running can install or service the aircon in your house.
