Office air-conditioning

How we approach it

Office aircons run long hours in Durban's heat and humidity. Dirty filters and coils mean weak airflow, damp musty air and higher power bills; blocked drains mean drips on desks and carpets.

We size and install new units for offices, and keep existing ones running with scheduled servicing, planned so we disturb your staff as little as possible.

Signs you need us

  • Some rooms too hot, others too cold
  • Musty smell when the aircon starts
  • Water dripping from indoor units
  • Noisy or rattling units in meeting rooms
  • Server or comms room running hot
  • No service in the last six months

What we repair

  • New office aircon installations
  • Six-monthly servicing of all units
  • Drain clearing and leak repairs
  • Gas leak repairs and regas
  • Fan, sensor and PCB repairs
  • Server room cooling checks

Questions

Offices: common questions

How often should office aircons be serviced?

Every six months for units that run all working day.

Can you work after hours or on weekends?

Where possible we can plan servicing for quieter times. Ask when you book.

Can you service all units in our building in one visit?

Yes. Give us the number of units and we'll quote for one visit.
