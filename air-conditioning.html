Servicing

Servicing that keeps the cold air coming

In Durban’s heat and humidity, a dirty aircon cools poorly and costs more to run. A service gets it back to full performance.

  • Filters, indoor coil and fan cleaned
  • Outdoor unit cleaned and checked
  • Drain line cleared so water doesn’t drip inside
  • Gas pressure checked and topped up where needed

Questions

Air-conditioning questions

How often should an aircon be serviced?

Units that run all day in offices, shops and restaurants do best with a service every six months. A home unit is usually fine with one service a year.

Can you install a unit I bought myself?

Yes. Tell us the make and size, and where it’s going, and we’ll quote for the installation.

Do you work on homes as well as businesses?

Yes. We specialise in commercial work, but we also install, repair and service aircons in homes.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Chatsworth and work across Durban and the surrounding areas.
