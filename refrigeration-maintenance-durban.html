Scheduled refrigeration maintenance

How we approach it

Businesses that rely on refrigeration every day, such as butcheries, restaurants, supermarkets and food distributors, get the most from scheduled visits. We agree how often to come, based on how hard your equipment works and how dusty or hot the site is.

Each visit follows the same checklist, so problems show up as changes from last time: a coil getting dirtier, a pressure drifting, a fan drawing more current.

Signs you need us

  • Several fridges, coolers or cold rooms on one site
  • Breakdowns that keep happening in summer
  • Electricity bills creeping up
  • Equipment that runs 24/7
  • Food-safety records that need steady temperatures
  • No service done in the last six months

What we repair

  • Condenser and evaporator coil cleaning
  • Fan motors, drains and door seals checked
  • Gas pressures and temperatures recorded
  • Controllers and defrost settings checked
  • Electrical connections tightened and tested
  • A short report after each visit

Questions

Refrigeration maintenance: common questions

How often should commercial refrigeration be maintained?

Typically every three to six months. Busy, dusty or hot sites need it more often. We'll suggest a schedule after the first visit.

Can you maintain equipment you didn't install?

Yes. We maintain all makes and models.

Is it worth it for one or two fridges?

For a single cooler, a condenser clean every few months is often enough, and we can show you how. Scheduled visits pay off most with several units or a cold room.
