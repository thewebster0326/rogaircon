Restaurant & bar refrigeration

How we approach it

Restaurant kitchens and bars are hot, busy and cramped, which is hard on refrigeration. Units are built under counters next to grills, doors open every few seconds during service, and condensers clog with grease and dust.

We work on cold rooms, upright and under-counter prep fridges, underbar fridges, back-bar coolers and the air-conditioning that keeps the dining room comfortable.

Signs you need us

  • Prep fridges warm during busy service
  • Underbar fridge cutting out on its overload
  • Cold room struggling after deliveries
  • Water leaking under counters
  • Greasy, clogged condensers
  • Dining room aircon not coping on hot nights

What we repair

  • Cold room and prep fridge repairs
  • Underbar and back-bar cooler repairs
  • Condenser cleaning, including greasy kitchens
  • Gas leaks and regas
  • Dining room aircon servicing
  • Work planned around service times

Questions

Restaurants & bars: common questions

Can you come before service?

Tell us your trading hours and we'll try to fit the repair in before you open.

Why do kitchen fridges fail so often?

Heat from cooking equipment and grease on the condenser make them work much harder. Regular coil cleaning helps a lot.

Do you service restaurant aircons too?

Yes, dining room and office aircons as well as refrigeration.
