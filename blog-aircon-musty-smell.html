Why Does My Aircon Smell Musty?

That damp, old-socks smell when the aircon starts isn't just unpleasant. It's usually mould growing inside the unit.

Where the smell comes from

Inside the indoor unit, the cold coil is wet whenever the aircon runs. Dust that gets past dirty filters sticks to that wet coil and the blower wheel, and in Durban's warmth and humidity it quickly grows mould and bacteria. When the aircon starts, it blows that smell into the room.

Common sources

  • Dirty or clogged filters
  • Mould on the indoor coil
  • A slimy blower wheel (the long fan inside)
  • Standing water in the drain tray
  • A blocked drain line

What you can do

Clean the filters every two to four weeks in summer. Run the aircon on fan-only for 10 to 15 minutes before switching off, to help dry the coil. That slows mould down, but it won't remove what's already growing deep in the coil and blower.

What a proper clean involves

A full service cleans the coil and blower wheel with coil cleaner, flushes the drain tray and line, and cleans or replaces filters. For offices and homes with people who have allergies or asthma, regular servicing makes a real difference to air quality.
