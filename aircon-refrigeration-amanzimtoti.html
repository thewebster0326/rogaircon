Amanzimtoti

Working in Amanzimtoti

Amanzimtoti and the coastal suburbs around it have restaurants, takeaways, shops and plenty of holiday accommodation. Summer brings peak trade and peak heat at the same time, which is exactly when struggling coolers and aircons give up.

Being on the coast, outdoor units here face constant salt air. Corroded coils and rusted electrical connections are common causes of breakdowns, and regular cleaning makes a big difference.

  • Aircons in holiday flats, guesthouses and homes
  • Restaurant and takeaway fridges and cold rooms
  • Coastal corrosion repairs on outdoor units
