Pinetown

Working in Pinetown

Pinetown mixes a busy retail centre with large industrial areas. That means two very different kinds of call: shopfront coolers and display fridges in town, and cold rooms, condensing units and factory office aircons in the industrial parks.

Industrial sites bring their own challenges, especially dust and fumes that coat condenser coils and push running pressures up. We clean and check those systems as part of every repair.

Pinetown jobs we do

  • Cold rooms and freezer rooms in food and distribution businesses
  • Condensing units and compressors on industrial sites
  • Office and boardroom split-unit aircons
  • Display fridges and coolers in Pinetown CBD shops

Cold room & freezer room repairs

A walk-in cold room or freezer room that won't hold temperature can cost a butchery, restaurant or supermarket a full room of stock overnight. We find the fault and get the room cold again.

Compressor & condensing unit repairs

The machinery behind the cold. We diagnose and repair compressors, condensing units, evaporators and controls on commercial refrigeration systems.

Aircon servicing & regas

Durban's humidity and summer heat make regular servicing the difference between an aircon that cools well for years and one that drips, smells and fails in December.

Industrial conditions

Condensers on factory roofs and walls in New Germany and Westmead collect dust and grime far faster than in a residential area. If your unit sits near a loading bay or extraction fan, plan on more frequent coil cleaning.
