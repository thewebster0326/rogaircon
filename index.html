Cold room & freezer room repairs

Cold room repairs

Bottle cooler repairs

Customers walk away from warm drinks. We repair single, double and triple-door glass bottle coolers in shops, spaza shops, bars and garage forecourts across Durban.

Bottle cooler repairs

Display fridge & deli counter repairs

Underbar & back-bar fridge repairs
