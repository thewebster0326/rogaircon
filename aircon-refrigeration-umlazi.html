Umlazi

Working in Umlazi

Umlazi is one of the biggest townships in the country, with a huge number of small businesses that run on refrigeration: bottle coolers in spaza shops and taverns, chest freezers, butchery cold rooms and display fridges.

Many of these coolers run flat out all day in hot shopfronts. Most of the breakdowns we see come from blocked condensers, tired fan motors and power surges, all of which are fixable.

Bottle cooler repairs

Supermarket & spaza refrigeration

From a single bottle cooler in a spaza shop to rows of multidecks and a cold room in a supermarket, we keep retail refrigeration cold and selling.

Fridge gas leak repairs & regas

A fridge that slowly stops cooling over weeks often has a gas leak. We find the leak, repair it, and recharge the system so it stays cold.

Fridge gas leaks

Aircon installation

New split-unit air-conditioning for offices, shops, restaurants and homes, sized for the room and installed neatly, with the system vacuumed and tested before we hand over.

Aircon installation

Power surges

Power coming back after an outage can surge and damage compressors and control boards. A surge protector on each cooler and switching off before power returns protects your equipment.
