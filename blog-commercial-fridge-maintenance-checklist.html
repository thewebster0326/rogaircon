Commercial Fridge Maintenance Checklist for Shops and Restaurants

Ten minutes of checks a week can prevent most commercial fridge breakdowns. Here's a simple checklist for your staff.

Weekly

  • Check and write down the temperature of each fridge, freezer and cold room
  • Make sure doors close and seal fully
  • Keep vents and fans inside the cabinet clear of stock
  • Wipe up any water under or around units

Monthly

  • Clean door gaskets with warm soapy water and check for splits
  • Check for ice building up on evaporator coils
  • Make sure nothing is stacked against the condenser or outdoor unit
  • Listen for new noises from fans and compressors

Every three months

  • Clean the condenser coil (switch off at the plug first); this is the single most important task
  • Check drain lines and trays are clear
  • Check strip curtains and door closers on cold rooms

Twice a year: professional service

A technician should check gas pressures, electrical connections, defrost settings and controllers, and clean coils properly. Busy sites with cold rooms benefit from more frequent visits.
