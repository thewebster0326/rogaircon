Umhlanga

Working in Umhlanga

Umhlanga's restaurant strip and the office towers and malls on Umhlanga Ridge depend on refrigeration and air-conditioning running all day. Restaurants need cold rooms, underbar fridges and display units working through every service; offices need dependable cooling in a hot, humid climate.

Close to the sea, outdoor units and condenser coils corrode faster than they do inland. We check for corrosion on every visit and clean coils with that in mind.

Salt air and outdoor units

Sea air leaves salt on condenser fins. Left alone, it corrodes the coil until it leaks gas. Rinsing and cleaning coastal outdoor units more often is the cheapest way to make them last.

Umhlanga: common questions

Why do aircons near the beach fail sooner?

Salt in the air corrodes the outdoor coil and electrical parts. Regular cleaning and inspection slows it down a lot.

Do you work on restaurant refrigeration in Umhlanga?

Yes: cold rooms, underbar fridges, display units and prep fridges.
