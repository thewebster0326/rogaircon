Underbar & back-bar fridge repairs

How we approach it

Underbar fridges live in the worst spot for refrigeration: tucked under a counter with hot equipment nearby and very little space for the condenser to breathe. Most failures we see start with poor ventilation and a clogged condenser.

We repair stainless steel under-counter fridges, glass-door back-bar coolers and prep fridges, and we'll tell you if the unit needs better ventilation to stop the fault coming back.

Signs you need us

  • Fridge warm during busy service, fine when quiet
  • Compressor cutting out on its overload
  • Hot air trapped behind the counter
  • Doors not sealing or springing open
  • Fridge noisy or vibrating against the bar
  • Water under the counter from a blocked drain

What we repair

  • Condenser cleaning and ventilation advice
  • Fan motors and condenser fans
  • Thermostats and controllers
  • Door seals, hinges and door closers
  • Gas leaks and regas
  • Start relays, overloads and compressors

Questions

Underbar fridge repairs: common questions

Why does my underbar fridge only struggle when the bar is busy?

Busy service means doors opening constantly and more heat behind the counter. A unit with a dirty condenser or poor ventilation copes when it's quiet and falls behind when it's busy.

Can you come before we open?

Tell us your trading hours when you book and we'll try to fit the repair in before service.

Do you also repair glass-front back-bar coolers?

Yes, back-bar coolers and wine fridges as well as stainless under-counter units.
