Servicing

Regular servicing keeps equipment cooling properly, cuts power use and extends its life.

  • Condenser and evaporator coils cleaned
  • Fans, drains and door seals checked
  • Gas pressures and temperatures checked
  • Electrical connections and controls tested

Before we arrive

If a cold room or fridge has stopped cooling:

  • Keep the door shut to hold the cold in
  • Move high-value stock to another unit if you can
  • Check the plug, breaker and thermostat setting
  • WhatsApp us a photo of the controller and unit sticker

Questions

Refrigeration questions

Do you repair all makes of commercial fridge?

Yes. We repair and service all makes and models of bottle coolers, display fridges, underbar fridges, cold rooms and refrigeration units.

How often should commercial refrigeration be serviced?

Equipment that runs around the clock, like cold rooms and bottle coolers, does best with a service every three to six months, depending on how hard it works and how dusty the area is.

Do you work outside Chatsworth?

Yes. We’re based in Chatsworth and work across Durban and the surrounding areas.

Can you install a new cold room or cooler?

Yes. We install new refrigeration equipment as well as repairing and servicing existing units. Send us the details and we’ll quote.
