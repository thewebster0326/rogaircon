Repair or Replace? When an Old Aircon or Fridge Isn't Worth Fixing

Not every breakdown means buying new. But some repairs just delay the inevitable. Here's how we help customers decide.

Usually worth repairing

  • Fan motors, capacitors, relays and thermostats
  • Sensors and many control board faults
  • Blocked drains and dirty coils
  • Accessible gas leaks on a unit in good condition

Time to think about replacing

  • The compressor has failed on an old unit
  • The coil is badly corroded and leaking in several places
  • The repair costs a large share of a new unit's price
  • The unit has needed several repairs in a short time
  • It's an old model using a refrigerant that's being phased out and is hard to get

Don't forget running costs

An old, tired aircon or fridge often uses much more electricity than a new, efficient one. If a unit runs all day, the power saving from replacing it can cover a good part of the cost over a few years.

An honest opinion

When we diagnose a fault, we'll tell you what the repair involves and whether we think it's worth it on that unit. If replacement makes more sense, we'll explain why and help you choose the right size.
