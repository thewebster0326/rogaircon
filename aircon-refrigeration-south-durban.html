South Durban industrial areas

Working in South Durban industrial areas

The South Durban industrial belt is full of food processors, distributors, warehouses and workshops. Many of them depend on cold rooms, freezer rooms and condensing units running around the clock, and on air-conditioning in offices, canteens and control rooms.

Heavy industry and port traffic mean dust, grime and in some spots salt air, all of which clog and corrode condenser coils. We clean and check coils as part of every repair, not only when it's asked for.

South Durban work we do

  • Cold rooms and freezer rooms in food and distribution sites
  • Compressors, condensing units and controls
  • Office, canteen and control-room aircons
  • Coil cleaning on dusty industrial sites
