Butchery refrigeration

How we approach it

Butcheries put refrigeration under more strain than most businesses: heavy loads of warm product going into cold rooms, doors opening all day, and display counters that must keep meat cold while looking good.

We look after the full chain, from the walk-in cold room and freezer room to the curved-glass display counters and the condensing units outside.

Signs you need us

  • Cold room not pulling down after a delivery
  • Ice building up on evaporators
  • Meat display drying out or warming at the front
  • Freezer room alarms at night
  • Condensing units struggling on hot days
  • Door seals and strip curtains worn out

What we repair

  • Cold room and freezer room repairs
  • Meat display counter repairs
  • Condensing unit and compressor repairs
  • Defrost, fan and controller faults
  • Door seals and strip curtains
  • Scheduled maintenance visits

Questions

Butcheries: common questions

What temperature should a butchery cold room run at?

Fresh meat is usually held between about 0 and 4 degrees Celsius, and frozen stock at minus 18 or colder. We set and check controllers to keep you in range.

Can you help after hours if the freezer room fails?

Yes, call us. Refrigeration emergencies where stock is at risk get priority.

Do you work on display counters too?

Yes: curved-glass meat displays, deli counters and serve-over counters.
